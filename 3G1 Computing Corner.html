Singapore's story

From a small trading port to a nation turning 61.

  1. 1819

    Raffles arrives

    In January, Sir Stamford Raffles of the British East India Company lands on the island and sets up a trading post. Ships from all over Asia start stopping here.

  2. 1959

    Our own government

    Singapore becomes self-governing. Lee Kuan Yew becomes Singapore's first Prime Minister, a job he holds until 1990. The state flag is raised for the first time in December.

  3. 1963

    Joining Malaysia

    Singapore joins Malaysia, together with Sabah and Sarawak.

  4. 1965

    Independence

    On 9 August 1965, Singapore separates from Malaysia and becomes an independent country. That's why National Day is on 9 August.

  5. 1966

    The first parade

    Singapore celebrates its first National Day with a parade at the Padang, a year after independence.

  6. 1972

    The Merlion

    The Merlion statue is unveiled at the mouth of the Singapore River. Half lion, half fish, it stars in Quraish's game.

  7. 1981

    A national flower

    The Vanda Miss Joaquim, a purple orchid first bred here in 1893 by Agnes Joaquim, is chosen as Singapore's national flower.

  8. 2026

    Happy 61st birthday!

    Singapore turns 61 this year. Count the flags in Quraish's game.

What we do in 3G1 Computing

G1 Computing is a hands-on subject. Most lessons happen at a computer, and here are some of the things you'd draw, create and learn.

draw

Vector logos and mascots

Shape lines with nodes and paths. Make them as big as a banner and they stay sharp.

Photo editing

Crop, resize and fix the brightness and contrast of a dull photo, pixel by pixel.

Game sprites

Draw your own characters, like the hand-drawn Merlion and soldier in our games.

create

Scratch games

Plan a game on a storyboard, then code the sprites, score and levels with blocks.

Videos

Turn photos and clips into a video with titles, transitions and music.

Spreadsheets and charts

Plan a class party budget. Formulas add it all up and a chart shows where the money goes.

Posters and reports

Lay out pages in Word with pictures, text boxes and columns.

Web forms and simple websites

Collect your classmates' opinions with an online form, and build a website from a template.

learn

Inside a computer

What the processor, memory and storage do, and how bytes grow into gigabytes and terabytes.

Networks and the cloud

How routers link devices, the difference between a LAN and the internet, and where cloud files really live.

Staying safe online

Spot scams, know your viruses from your ransomware, make strong passwords and back up your work.
